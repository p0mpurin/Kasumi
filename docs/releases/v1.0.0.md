Kasumi 1.0: three ways to play, one game hub.

- Steam Link: play your own PC's games, streamed over your Wi-Fi. Pair with a code you type into Steam once, no NVIDIA account needed. Big Picture, your desktop, your recently played games, and non-Steam games you added to Steam
- Steam extras: the PC's mouse from the right stick (with the pointer shown on the desktop), typing on the PC from the on-screen or phone keyboard, up to four paired PCs (press Y in the Steam library), and Quit game closes the game on the PC
- Xbox Cloud Gaming (beta): sign in with your Microsoft account and play Game Pass and free games like Fortnite at 60 fps, with your place in the queue shown
- A new home: every service is a painted card on the game hub. Press A and the card grows into its world; B brings you back. Pick a service on first start, and only that one asks you to sign in
- Your library is now a shelf of covers. The focused game glows behind it, covers load around what you're looking at first, and they arrive much faster
- Continue from the hub: your last game is right there, one press of START away
- New game page and launch screen, and a celebration when you sign in or pair
- Sharper text everywhere, clearer buttons, and sign-in screens that say what went wrong
- Fewer freezes: slow SD cards no longer stall the menus or games, frozen video on weak Wi-Fi recovers by itself, and reconnects after a drop work more often

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.

Kasumi is unofficial and not made by NVIDIA, Microsoft or Valve.
