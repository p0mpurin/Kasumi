Kasumi 1.0.3: fixes the Xbox crash from 1.0.2.

- Fixed a crash when starting Xbox games other than Fortnite. 1.0.2 tried to decode their 720p stream, and the 3DS couldn't handle it. Those games now show a clear message instead: they only stream in 720p, which the 3DS can't show yet
- Fortnite on Xbox Cloud Gaming works as before
- Kept from 1.0.2: no waiting music over the game's sound, and fewer false decoder errors in reports

Sorry about 1.0.2. Update from Settings > Updates, or install Kasumi.cia with FBI. Homebrew Launcher users can replace Kasumi.3dsx. Your login, library and settings are kept.
