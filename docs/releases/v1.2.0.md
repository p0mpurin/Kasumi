Kasumi 1.2.0: a guided start, a touch mouse for Steam Link, a magnifier and a winter theme.

- A short tour on first start: language, Japanese labels, button layout and names. START skips it
- Settings > System > Japanese labels: hide the Japanese under button names
- Steam Link picture mode: press MOUSE twice, then tap the game to click, hold to right-click, C-Stick scrolls. B right-clicks in mouse mode
- Magnifier: Settings > Picture > Lower screen panel shows a minimap or small text bigger
- 3D slider: new Camera speed and Dim lower screen choices
- Winter theme Yuki, and snow in every theme from 1 December to 7 January. Each theme now colours the home screen with its own weather: petals, leaves, rain, fireflies or gold dust
- A cleaner look: What's new cards after an update, a shorter tour, game pages with play time at a glance, and quieter buttons so the main one stands out
- Fortnite on Xbox no longer stops with "only streams in 720p"
- A second game in one run no longer fails with "video decoder stopped responding"
- Clearer messages when Steam Link can't find your PC (firewall ports) or a school or work Wi-Fi blocks a service

Update from Settings > Updates, or install Kasumi.cia with FBI. Your login, library and settings are kept.
