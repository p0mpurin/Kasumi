Kasumi 1.1.0: Kasumi in your language.

- New in Settings > System > Language: English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. The whole menu changes: buttons, settings and their help, errors and notices, controller button names, the phone keyboard and the search keyboard
- Japanese needs a 3DS whose system font has Japanese characters (Japanese consoles); other consoles stay in English for that choice
- Game and service names stay as they are. If something reads oddly or doesn't fit, send a report or tell us on Discord

Update from Settings > Updates, or install Kasumi.cia with FBI. Homebrew Launcher users can replace Kasumi.3dsx. Your login, library and settings are kept.

Kasumi is unofficial and not made by NVIDIA, Microsoft or Valve.
