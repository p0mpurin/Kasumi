Kasumi 1.0.2: Xbox games beyond Fortnite.

- Xbox Cloud Gaming: games other than Fortnite played their sound over a black screen. They stream at 720p, which Kasumi now decodes and fits to the top screen. If your 3DS can't show a game, Kasumi now says so instead of leaving the screen black. Please send a report (Settings > Send diagnostic report) and tell us on Discord which game you tried
- Kasumi's waiting music no longer plays over the game's sound while the picture is starting
- Fewer false "decoder" errors in reports: the video decoder now always starts on a full picture, for every service

Update from Settings > Updates, or install Kasumi.cia with FBI. Homebrew Launcher users can replace Kasumi.3dsx. Your login, library and settings are kept.
