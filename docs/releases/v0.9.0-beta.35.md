The biggest update so far: new control options, voice chat, a phone keyboard and a much better 60 fps mode.

- Xbox mode: button prompts can show Xbox names (A/B/X/Y) as well as PlayStation
- Custom button mapping: remap any button, plus combos, turbo, toggle and stick outputs
- L3 and R3 are now on the touch screen while you play
- Aim assist: Kasumi now shows up as a real Xbox controller, so games that give controllers aim assist should treat you as one
- Mouse mode stays responsive on weak Wi-Fi
- Voice chat: turn on Settings > Sound & Look > Microphone to talk in games, then tap MIC on the touch screen to mute
- Phone keyboard: scan the QR code and type with your phone instead of the 3DS keyboard
- 60 fps (beta) no longer drops back to 30, with less input delay, smoother frame pacing and a sharper picture. Very heavy games can still hitch at 60; Kasumi suggests 30 when that happens
- Zooming no longer adds lag, and panning follows your finger smoothly
- Faster menus: B in Settings goes back right away, and the in-game menu responds instantly
- Saving settings and play history no longer freezes the game for a moment
- Clearer messages when something goes wrong

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
