Fixes the installed CIA freezing the whole console when a stream starts (beta.5).

- Fixed: the CIA froze at "input ready" as the video started, and only holding POWER got out of it. The CIA never asked the system to load the hardware video decoder, so Kasumi waited for it forever. It is now loaded with the app, and if the decoder is ever missing Kasumi shows an error instead of freezing
- Includes everything from beta.4 and beta.5: resume after a crash, queue alert, Continue, QR install, the audio fix
