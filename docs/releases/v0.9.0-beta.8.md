Close the lid mid-game without losing your session.

- New: closing the lid now pauses instead of sleeping. The screens turn off and the sound and controls are held, but Kasumi stays connected, so opening the lid puts you straight back in with no reconnect. A short "Welcome back" card shows how long you were away
- Settings > Closing the lid has three choices: Pause (new default), Sleep (saves the most battery, reconnects on opening) and Keep playing (screens off, game and sound keep running)
- Why: sleeping dropped the connection, and after reconnecting NVIDIA could end the session a few minutes later
- Pause keeps Wi-Fi on, so a long pause uses more battery than Sleep

Update from Settings > Updates, or install with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR Code). Your login, library and settings are kept.
