Smarter reconnects when Wi-Fi drops, including with the lid closed.

- Fixed: the 3DS turns Wi-Fi off a couple of seconds after the lid closes, even while Kasumi keeps it awake. Kasumi then used up all three reconnect attempts in a few seconds on "Couldn't resolve host name" and showed an error. It now waits: while the lid is shut it shows "Paused with the lid closed", and after opening it waits for Wi-Fi to come back (up to 30 s) before reconnecting with all three attempts
- The same wait applies when Wi-Fi drops mid-game, for example out of range of the router
- Experiment: when Wi-Fi goes off with the lid shut, Kasumi asks the system to turn it back on (like the HOME Menu's wireless switch). If that works, the stream stays connected through the pause
- The "Welcome back" card only says "still connected" when the connection really survived
