Kasumi 1.0.4: smoother GeForce NOW and Steam Link, and the 3D slider joins in.

- New: the 3D slider can zoom or press a button in games (New 3DS and New 3DS XL). Settings > Controls > 3D slider: Zoom (slide up for three steps, all the way down for the whole picture) or a button of your choice (L3, R3, Guide, triggers and more), held while the slider is past halfway. Thanks to the players who suggested it
- The FPS readout now counts the frames you actually see. It used to count frames decoded, so a picture that stuttered could still read "30"
- Steam Link: fixed packets that were never resent after Wi-Fi hiccups (a timing mix-up could make the resend timer minutes long), and packets the PC never answers are no longer resent for the whole session. Fewer freezes and less Wi-Fi traffic
- GeForce NOW: the last reconnect attempt after a Wi-Fi drop now gets the time NVIDIA needs (about 20 s) instead of giving up at 8 s
- Fewer false problem reports during normal play
- Xbox Cloud Gaming is paused for maintenance. Every game except Fortnite streams at a size the 3DS can't show yet. Testers have already got 720p playing on a 3DS, and Xbox comes back once it runs smoothly. Your Microsoft login is kept

Update from Settings > Updates, or install Kasumi.cia with FBI. Homebrew Launcher users can replace Kasumi.3dsx. Your login, library and settings are kept.
