Kasumi 1.0.4: Fortnite on Xbox starts reliably again.

- Fixed Fortnite on Xbox Cloud Gaming sometimes ending with "only streams in 720p" before its picture came up. Fortnite can take up to about 11 seconds to switch to the 3DS's screen size, and 1.0.3 stopped waiting after 6
- Xbox games that stream at a size the 3DS can't show now say so after 15 seconds, including games that send more than 720p (like Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2), instead of staying on a black screen
